This fixture exists to demonstrate long-form kitchen-note typography and the editorial status system. It does not describe a cooking session that actually happened.

## The question a real note would answer

When a generated idea sounds plausible but tastes flat, how do you distinguish “needs more salt” from “needs contrast”? A publishable note would document the starting point, the changes, and the result with enough precision to be useful.

## What would count as evidence

A future entry should record amounts, sequence, sensory observations, and what the next cook ought to do differently. “AI suggested it” is provenance, not proof.
