## Preparation

1. Fill a large balloon glass or wine glass generously with ice.
2. Add the gin.
3. Express the orange peel over the glass and add it.
4. Slowly pour in the chilled tonic.
5. Stir gently once.
6. Lightly slap the rosemary sprig to release its aromas and garnish.

## Tasting Notes

Exceptionally aromatic, with bright citrus and herbal character, a crisp finish, and restrained sweetness.
