## Preparation

1. Chill a coupe or martini glass.
2. Add the vodka, cold brew, Kahlúa, and heavy cream to a cocktail shaker.
3. Fill with ice and shake vigorously for about 15 seconds, until very cold and lightly aerated.
4. Double-strain into the chilled glass.
5. Garnish with coffee beans if desired.

## Tasting Notes

Designed to be coffee-forward and silky rather than milkshake-like. Kahlúa brings sweetness, while the cream softens the coffee's bitterness and complements a rich chocolate dessert. No additional syrup is included.

## Variations to Test

- **Lighter:** Replace the 0.5 oz heavy cream with 0.25 oz heavy cream plus 0.25 oz whole milk.
- **Regular-strength cold brew:** Try 1.5 oz cold brew and reduce vodka to 1.5 oz if the coffee flavor is too faint.
- **Sweeter:** Taste before adding any extra sweetener; Kahlúa and a chocolate ganache cake already contribute sweetness.

## Experiment Notes

Proposed as a dessert cocktail for a 14th anniversary dinner, paired with chocolate ganache cake after Champagne and scallops. The initial suggestion used a half-and-half equivalent (heavy cream plus whole milk); the richer heavy-cream version was chosen for dessert service. Final tasting feedback and any actual adjustments have not yet been recorded. Keep this experimental until the finished drink is reviewed.
