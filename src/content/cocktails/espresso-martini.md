## Preparation

1. Chill a coupe or martini glass.
2. Combine vodka, strong cold brew (or cooled espresso), and Kahlúa in a cocktail shaker.
3. For a creamier dessert drink, add **up to 0.25 oz cream**; omit it for the classic coffee-forward version.
4. Fill the shaker with ice and shake vigorously for about 15 seconds until thoroughly chilled and aerated.
5. Double-strain into the chilled glass. Garnish with coffee beans if desired.

## Tasting Notes

Kahlúa provides sweetness, while concentrated coffee gives the drink its backbone. Optional cream softens bitterness and adds a silky finish, particularly with chocolate desserts. No extra sweetener is included by default.

## Recipe History and Testing

Previously tracked as separate `Espresso Martini` and `Creamy Cold Brew Martini` entries. These are now a single recipe with optional cream, not two separate cocktails. Strong cold brew and espresso are both acceptable coffee bases. The creamy variation was considered for a November 10, 2026 anniversary dessert alongside chocolate ganache cake. An earlier draft called for 0.5 oz heavy cream, with a possible half-and-half-style substitute; the current lighter optional addition is 0.25 oz. Exact final tasting feedback for this combined specification has not been recorded, so it remains experimental.
