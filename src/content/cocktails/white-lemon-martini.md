## Preparation

1. Chill a martini or coupe glass.
2. Add vodka, Cointreau, fresh lemon juice, and simple syrup to a shaker filled with ice.
3. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds until thoroughly chilled.
4. Double-strain into the chilled glass, leaving all ice behind.
5. Garnish with a lemon twist. Do not sugar the rim.

## Simple Syrup

Combine equal volumes granulated sugar and water (for example, 0.5 cup of each) in a saucepan. Warm gently and stir just until the sugar dissolves. Cool completely before using and refrigerate any remainder.

## Tasting Notes

A tart, clean citrus cocktail with orange aromatics from Cointreau and just enough syrup to round out the lemon. Served up in a cold glass; no ice or sugar rim in the finished drink.
