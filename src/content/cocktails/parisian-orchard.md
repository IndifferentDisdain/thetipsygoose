## Preparation

1. Combine all ingredients in a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake vigorously for 12–15 seconds.
3. Double-strain into a chilled martini glass.
4. Garnish with a thin apple slice or lemon twist if desired.

No additional syrup is needed in the tested specification.

## Kitchen review — Version 1

First tested November 18, 2026, alongside Poulet à la Moutarde. The house VIP rated it **7.5/10** and requested less elderflower and more apple liqueur.

## Proposed Version 2 — Not yet tested

- 1½ oz vodka
- 1¼ oz Berentzen apple liqueur
- ¼ oz St-Germain
- ½ oz fresh lemon juice

Use the same shaking and straining technique. The proposed change shifts the balance toward apple while retaining the original total volume and lemon quantity. The structured ingredients above represent the actual tested first version, not the untested revision.

The Parisian Orchard remains experimental pending another tasting.
