## Preparation

1. Gently press the mint leaves in a shaker with the agave and a splash of citrus juice. Avoid aggressively grinding the leaves.
2. Add tequila, orange juice, lime juice and Grand Marnier along with the remaining citrus juice. Stir briefly if needed to dissolve the agave.
3. Add ice and shake vigorously for 10–15 seconds.
4. Double-strain through a cocktail strainer and a fine-mesh sieve into two rocks glasses over fresh ice.
5. Garnish with orange or mint if desired. A half-salted rim is optional.

## Optional make-ahead

For convenient dinner service, mix the tequila, orange juice, lime juice, Grand Marnier and agave in advance, stir well, cover and refrigerate. Wait until serving to introduce the mint and ice, then shake and double-strain as usual. Advance chilling is entirely optional; the cocktail can be made immediately from fresh ingredients.
