## Preparation

1. Chill a cocktail or martini glass.
2. Add the vodka, Berentzen Apple, and fresh lemon juice to a shaker with ice.
3. Shake until thoroughly chilled.
4. Strain into the prepared glass.
5. Add an apple garnish if desired.

## Tasting Notes

Crisp apple leads, while fresh lemon keeps the drink bright and prevents the fruit from reading as heavy or candied.

## Recipe History

The TiffTini is Tiffany’s signature apple martini. Berentzen Apple is the established ingredient.

Applejack was tested as a substitute and rejected; it is preserved here as experiment history, not an approved variation.
