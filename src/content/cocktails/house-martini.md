## Preparation

1. Chill a martini glass.
2. Combine the vodka and vermouth in a mixing glass with ice.
3. Add olive brine if desired.
4. Stir until thoroughly chilled and diluted.
5. Strain into the chilled glass.
6. Garnish with olives.

## House Rules

- Use olives, not cocktail onions.
- Olive brine is optional and adjustable.
- The standard spirit-to-vermouth ratio is 2.5:0.5.
- This is a vodka martini, not a gin martini—and it is not a Gibson.

## Tasting Notes

Clean, very cold, and spirit-forward, with dry vermouth giving the vodka shape and optional brine taking it in a more savory direction.
