instructionGroups:
  - name: Render pancetta and parboil potatoes
    steps:
      - "Put the diced pancetta in a cold cast-iron skillet. Cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, for approximately 6–8 minutes until the fat renders and the pancetta is golden and crisp. Lift out the pancetta to drain and reserve for the salad. Leave about 1 tablespoon of rendered fat in the skillet."
      - "Meanwhile, bring a saucepan of water to a boil with 1 teaspoon kosher salt and 1/4 teaspoon baking soda. Add the 3/4-inch potato chunks and gently boil for about 6–8 minutes after the water returns to a boil, until the outsides are tender but the centers still offer some resistance."
      - "Drain and steam-dry the potatoes for 2–3 minutes, then shake the colander gently to rough up their surfaces. That starchy coating is what crisps in the pan."
  - name: Fry the potatoes
    steps:
      - "Heat the cast iron with the reserved pancetta fat over medium-high heat. Supplement with olive oil as needed to bring the total frying fat to about 1 1/2 tablespoons."
      - "Arrange the potatoes in a single layer and leave undisturbed for 3–4 minutes. Turn and pan-fry for approximately 7–10 minutes more, until browned and crisp on several sides."
      - "Reduce the heat to medium. Add 1/2 tablespoon butter and finely chopped rosemary during the final 1–2 minutes. Toss, add pepper, and taste before adding salt."
      - "Transfer to a rack or plate and hold uncovered so the surfaces stay crisp while you cook the chicken."
  - name: Prepare and sear the saltimbocca
    steps:
      - "Slice chicken breasts horizontally into thin cutlets and pound evenly to about 1/4–3/8 inch thick. Season the bare chicken side lightly with salt and pepper."
      - "Place 1–2 sage leaves on each cutlet and press a thin slice of prosciutto over them. Secure with a toothpick only if necessary. Just before cooking, lightly flour the exposed chicken side and shake off excess."
      - "Heat a stainless-steel skillet over medium heat with 1 tablespoon olive oil and 1 tablespoon butter. Place chicken prosciutto-side down first and cook about 2–3 minutes until lightly crisp and golden."
      - "Flip carefully and cook another 2–4 minutes, adjusting for thickness, until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Transfer to a warm plate, prosciutto-side up, loosely tent, and preserve the fond in the skillet."
  - name: Make the white wine-butter sauce
    steps:
      - "Reduce the chicken skillet to medium-low. Sweat minced shallot in the remaining fat for 1–2 minutes; add minced garlic for another 20–30 seconds."
      - "Add 1/2 cup dry white wine and scrape up the browned fond. Add 1/2 cup chicken stock and 2–3 sage leaves, then simmer until reduced to approximately 1/4–1/3 cup of liquid."
      - "Remove the pan from heat and whisk in 2 tablespoons cold cubed butter, a little at a time, to make a glossy sauce. Add lemon juice, taste, and adjust seasoning. Do not boil after mounting with butter."
  - name: Salad and plating
    steps:
      - "Just before serving, whisk extra-virgin olive oil with 1–2 teaspoons lemon juice and black pepper; lightly dress the arugula. Shave Parmesan over the salad."
      - "Lay the chicken cutlets slightly overlapping, prosciutto-side up. Spoon the pan sauce around and partly beneath the chicken to preserve the prosciutto texture."
      - "Cluster crispy rosemary potatoes on one side, add a small mound of arugula salad on the other, and scatter a restrained amount of crispy pancetta over the greens. Finish with Parmesan shavings."
